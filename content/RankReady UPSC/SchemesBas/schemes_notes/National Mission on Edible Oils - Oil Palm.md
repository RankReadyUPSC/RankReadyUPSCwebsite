## Salient Features

- Aims to expand oil palm cultivation to 10 lakh hectares and increase crude palm oil production to 11.2 lakh tonnes till 2025-26.
- Focuses on North-East (NE) India and the Andaman & Nicobar Islands.
- Beneficiaries: Farmers in NE and Andaman Islands.
- Implementing Authority: Department of Horticulture.
