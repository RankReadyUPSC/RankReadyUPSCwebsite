## Salient Features

- Provides 100 Mbps broadband connectivity to all Gram Panchayats (GPs).
- Utilizes GSAT-11 and GSAT-19 for inaccessible areas.
- Recognized as a project of national importance; the network is a national asset.
- Beneficiaries: Gram Panchayats.
- Implementing Authority: BSNL.
- Previous Name: National Optical Fibre Network.
- Initiative: National Broadband Mission.
