## Salient Features

- Provides financial assistance to senior citizen homes and continuous care homes under IPSrC.  Supplies aids and assistive devices to senior citizens through the Rashtriya Vayoshri Yojana.
- Supports skilling and livelihood initiatives for senior citizens.  Beneficiaries: Senior citizens.   Tools: SACRED Portal, SAGE Portal.
