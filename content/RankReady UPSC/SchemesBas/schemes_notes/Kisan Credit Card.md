## Salient Features

- Offers collateral-free loans up to ₹1.6 lakh for agriculture.
- Provides a credit line for agriculture and allied activities with interest subvention of 3% for prompt repayment.
- Beneficiaries: Farmers, SHGs, JLGs.
- Implementing Authority: Commercial Banks, RRBs, Cooperatives.
