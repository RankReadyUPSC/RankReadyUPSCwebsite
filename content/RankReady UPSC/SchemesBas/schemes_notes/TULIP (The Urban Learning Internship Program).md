## Salient Features

- Offers internships to students and recent graduates (within 18 months of graduation) in ULBs, Smart Cities, and state/UT parastatals.
- Beneficiaries: Graduates of undergraduate programs.
- Implementing Authority: NSDC
- Tool: Part of the Smart Cities Mission.
