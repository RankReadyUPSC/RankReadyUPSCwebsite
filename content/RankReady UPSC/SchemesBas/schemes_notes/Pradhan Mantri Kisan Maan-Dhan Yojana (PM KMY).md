## Salient Features

- Provides an assured pension of ₹3,000/month for farmers attaining the age of 60 years.
- Monthly contributions range from ₹55-₹200.
- Spouses receive 50% pension in case of the subscriber's death.
- Beneficiaries: Farmers aged 18-40 with land up to 2 ha.
- Implementing Authority: LIC.
