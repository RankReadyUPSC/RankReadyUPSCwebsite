## Salient Features

- Provides a guaranteed pension for senior citizens above 60 years for 10 years.
- Pension ranges from ₹1,000 to ₹12,000 per month based on contributions.
- Beneficiaries: Senior citizens (above 60 years).
- Implementing Authority: LIC
