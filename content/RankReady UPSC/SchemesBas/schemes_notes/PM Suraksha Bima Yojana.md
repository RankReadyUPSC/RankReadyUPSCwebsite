## Salient Features

- Provides accidental insurance: ₹2 lakh for accidental death or total permanent disability, and ₹1 lakh for partial permanent disability.
- Available for individuals aged 18-70 years with a savings bank or post office account.  Beneficiaries: All eligible individuals in the specified age group.
- Implementing Authority: General Insurance Companies.
