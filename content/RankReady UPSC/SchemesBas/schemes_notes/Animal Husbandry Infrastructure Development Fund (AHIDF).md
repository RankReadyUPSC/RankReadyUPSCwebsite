## Salient Features

- Total outlay: ₹29,610 crore, including ₹15,000 crore AHIDF.
- Loans: Up to 90% of the project cost.
- Credit guarantee of ₹750 crore provided by the Government of India, covering up to 25% of the term loan for MSMEs and dairy cooperatives.
- Beneficiaries: FPOs, MSMEs, Section-8 companies, entrepreneurs, and private firms.  Implementing Authority: NABARD, Respective Departments.
