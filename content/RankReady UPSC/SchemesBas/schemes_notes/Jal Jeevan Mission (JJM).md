## Salient Features

- Aims to provide functional household tap connections (FHTC) for all.
- Focuses on water quality monitoring, long-term drinking water security, and WASH initiatives.  Goa and Burhanpur District (Madhya Pradesh) certified as “Har Ghar Jal.”
- Beneficiaries: Rural and urban households.
- Implementing Authority: State Water and Sanitation Mission.
