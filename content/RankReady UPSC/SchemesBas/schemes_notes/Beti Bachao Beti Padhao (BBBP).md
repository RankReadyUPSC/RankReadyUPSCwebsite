## Salient Features

- Promotes behavioral and social change to improve the status of girls.
- Capacity building for frontline workers, medical practitioners, and district officers.
- Guddi-Gudda boards displayed in Gram Panchayats.
- Beneficiaries: All districts.  Implementing Authority: Committee under Mission Shakti.
