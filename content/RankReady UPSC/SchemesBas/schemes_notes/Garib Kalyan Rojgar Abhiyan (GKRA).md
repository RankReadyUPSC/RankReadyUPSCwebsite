## Salient Features

- Focuses on migrant workers and unemployed rural citizens in six states (Bihar, UP, MP, Rajasthan, Odisha, Jharkhand).
- Provides free food grains to 81.35 crore NFSA beneficiaries.
- Beneficiaries: Migrant workers and rural citizens.
- Implementing Authority: Respective Departments.
