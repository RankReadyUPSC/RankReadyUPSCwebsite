## Salient Features

- Provides zero customs duty on import of capital goods for export production.
- Under FTP2023, offers additional benefits to sectors like textiles, dairy, green hydrogen, and BEVs.
- Beneficiaries: Manufacturer exporters, merchant exporters, and service providers.  Implementing Authority: Directorate General of Foreign Trade (DGFT).
