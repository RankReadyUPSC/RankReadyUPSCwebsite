## Salient Features

- Allows duty-free import of raw materials for manufacturing export items as per Standard Input-Output Norms (SION).
- Beneficiaries: Manufacturers, exporters, and service providers.
- Implementing Authority: Directorate General of Foreign Trade (DGFT).
