## Salient Features

- Aims to stabilize supply chain infrastructure for perishable crops.
- Includes long-term interventions for value chain development and short-term interventions for fruits and vegetables under the "TOP to TOTAL" approach.
- Beneficiaries: Farmers, FPOs, cooperatives, and food processors.
