## Salient Features

- Provides housing for all by 2024.
- Grants of ₹1.2 lakh (plain areas) and ₹1.3 lakh (hilly areas, NE, UTs).
- Converges with MGNREGA for unskilled wages and SBM for toilet construction.
- Beneficiaries: EWS, LIG, MIG categories.
- Tool: CPGRAMS (grievance redressal).
