## Salient Features

- Empowers MPs to develop five Adarsh Grams (one per year) by 2024.
- MPs identify Gram Panchayats, prepare village development plans, and monitor progress.  Beneficiaries: Selected villages (3000-5000 population in plains; 1000-3000 in hilly/tribal areas)
