## Salient Features

- Funds state governments/UTs for preventive education, awareness generation, capacity building, and vocational training for ex-drug addicts.
- Programmes aim to reduce drug demand.
- Beneficiaries: Drug-affected individuals, families, and institutions.
- Key Initiative: Nasha Mukt Bharat Abhiyaan (NMBA).
