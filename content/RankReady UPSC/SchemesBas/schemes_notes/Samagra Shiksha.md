## Salient Features

- Overarching programme covering pre-school to class 12 education.
- Focuses on digital education, upgrading Kasturba Gandhi Balika Vidyalayas (KGBVs), and implementing UDISE+
- Subsumes Sarva Shiksha Abhiyan, Rashtriya Madhyamik Shiksha Abhiyan (RMSA), and Scheme for Teacher Education.
- Beneficiaries: Students and schools under the education sector.
- Implementing Authority: State Implementation Society.
