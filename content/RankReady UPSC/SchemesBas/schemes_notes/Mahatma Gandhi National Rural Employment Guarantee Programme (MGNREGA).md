## Salient Features

- Guarantees 100 days of employment per year for registered households.
- Fund sharing: Centre funds 100% of unskilled labor costs and 75% of material costs, while states fund 25% of material costs.
- Beneficiaries: Adult members of rural households.
- Tool: GeoMGNREGA (by ISRO and NIC).
