## Salient Features

- Develops SC-dominated villages into ‘Adarsh Grams.’
- Provides grants-in-aid for socio-economic betterment projects for SCs and constructs/repairs educational hostels for SC students.
- Beneficiaries: SC citizens in villages with more than 40% SC population.
- Implementing Authority: Central Management Information System.
- Subsumed Schemes: SCA to SCSP, BJRCY, PMAGY.
