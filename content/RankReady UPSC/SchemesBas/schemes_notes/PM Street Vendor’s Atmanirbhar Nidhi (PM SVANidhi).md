## Salient Features

- Provides working capital loans with a 1-year tenure.
- Offers interest subsidies (7% p.a.) for timely repayment and cashback incentives (up to ₹100/month) for digital transactions.
- Beneficiaries: Street vendors with vending certificates or identity cards.
- Implementing Authority: SIDBI, Credit Guarantee Fund Trust.
- Tool: Street Vendors (Protection of Livelihood and Regulation of Street Vending) Act, 2014.
