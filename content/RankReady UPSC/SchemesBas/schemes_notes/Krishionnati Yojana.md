## Salient Features

- An umbrella scheme comprising 11 sub-schemes:  1. MIDH (Mission for Integrated Development of Horticulture).  2. NFSM (National Food Security Mission).  3. NMOOP (National Mission on Oilseeds and Oil Palm).  4. NMSA (National Mission for Sustainable Agriculture).  5. SMAM (Sub-Mission on Agricultural Mechanization).  6. SMPP (Sub-Mission on Plant Protection).  7. ISACES (Integrated Scheme on Agriculture Census and Economics).  8. ISAC (Integrated Scheme on Agricultural Cooperation).  9. ISAM (Integrated Scheme on Agricultural Marketing).  10. NeGPA (National e-Governance Plan in Agriculture).  11. RKVY (Rashtriya Krishi Vikas Yojana).
- Beneficiaries: Farmers under respective sub-schemes.
