## Salient Features

- USD 500 million loan from the World Bank; USD 308 million from GoI.
- Includes MSME Green Investment and Financing for Transformation (MSE-GIFT), MSE SPICE for circular economy promotion, and MSE ODR for online dispute resolution.
- Beneficiaries: MSMEs.
- Implementing Authority: National MSME Council.
