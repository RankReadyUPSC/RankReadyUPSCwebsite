## Salient Features

- Ensures Minimum Support Price (MSP) for notified crops like pulses, oilseeds, and copra. Includes three components: Price Support Scheme, Price Deficiency Payment Scheme, and Private Procurement Pilot Scheme.
- Beneficiaries: Farmers cultivating notified crops.
- Implementing Authority: Food Corporation of India (FCI), NAFED.
