## Salient Features

- Recruits Agniveers (17.5–21 years) for four years in the Army, Navy, and Air Force.
- Provides a Seva Nidhi package of ₹11.71 lakh (tax-free) after four years.
- Only 25% of Agniveers will continue for permanent commission (15 years).
- Beneficiaries: Agniveers.
