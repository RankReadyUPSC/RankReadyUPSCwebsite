## Salient Features

- Supports 2 lakh micro food processing units.
- Focuses on waste-to-wealth products, minor forest products, and aspirational districts.
- Provides seed capital of ₹40,000 per SHG member.
- Beneficiaries: FPOs, SHGs, cooperatives, and food processing entrepreneurs.
