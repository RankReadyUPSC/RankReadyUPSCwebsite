## Salient Features

- Supports integrated digital health infrastructure.
- Establishes ABHA numbers, Health Facility Registry (HFR), and Healthcare Professionals Registry (HPR).
- Beneficiaries: Citizens, healthcare professionals, and facilities.
- Implementing Authority: National Health Agency.
