## Salient Features

- Focuses on water budgeting and convergence with MGNREGS for efficient irrigation.
- Includes Long-Term Irrigation Fund and Micro Irrigation Fund.
- Beneficiaries: Farmers, farmer groups, FPOs, and watershed committees.
- Implementing Authority: Central Water Commission, National Steering Committee.
- Sub-Schemes: IWMP, AIBP, HKKP (Har Khet Ko Pani).
