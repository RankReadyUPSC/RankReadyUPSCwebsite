## Salient Features

- Focuses on securing livelihoods, conserving biodiversity, and restoring the Himalayan ecosystem.
- Funded by the Global Environment Facility (GEF) under the Global Wildlife Program.  Beneficiaries: Himalayan states.
- Implementing Authority: Respective Ministries, UNDP.
