## Salient Features

- Facilitates collateral-free loans ranging from ₹10 lakh to ₹1 crore.
- Focus on Greenfield Enterprises in manufacturing, services, trading, and allied activities.
- Special provisions for women and SC/ST entrepreneurs.
- Beneficiaries: Women, SC/ST entrepreneurs.
- Implementing Authority: NABARD, SIDBI.
