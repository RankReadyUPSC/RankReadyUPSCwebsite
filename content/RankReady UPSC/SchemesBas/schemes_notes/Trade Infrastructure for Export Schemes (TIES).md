## Salient Features

- Provides financial assistance for export-linked infrastructure development.
- Grants-in-aid: Up to 50% of project cost (general projects) and up to 80% (NE states, Himalayan states, or states with poor export infrastructure).
- Beneficiaries: Exporters, trade bodies, state/central agencies.
- Implementing Authority: Department of Commerce.
