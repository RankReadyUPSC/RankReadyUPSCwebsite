## Salient Features

- Addresses three pillars of rural startups: finances, incubation, and skills.
- Develops Community Resource Persons-Enterprise Promotion (CRP-EP) to support local entrepreneurs.
- Promotes Block Resource Centres (BRCs) to manage resources in SVEP blocks.
- Beneficiaries: Rural poor, women, SC/ST communities, and artisans.
