## Salient Features

- Maps the skills of Indian workers returning under the Vande Bharat Mission.
- Integrates with ASEEM (Aatmanirbhar Skilled Employee Employer Mapping) Portal.  Beneficiaries: Returning Indian citizens.
- Implementing Authority: National Skill Development Corporation (NSDC).
