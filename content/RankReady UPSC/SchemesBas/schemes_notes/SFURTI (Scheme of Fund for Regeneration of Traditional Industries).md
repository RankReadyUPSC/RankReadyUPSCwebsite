## Salient Features

- Organizes traditional industries and artisans into collectives.
- Formation of a Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV) for operating SFURTI clusters is mandatory.  Beneficiaries: Existing artisans from traditional industries.
- Implementing Authority: Khadi and Village Industries Commission (KVIC).
