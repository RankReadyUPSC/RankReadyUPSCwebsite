## Salient Features

- Brings one woman from each rural household into SHGs.
- Provides revolving funds of ₹10,000–₹15,000 for SHG members adhering to Panchasutra principles.
- Beneficiaries: Rural poor households identified through SECC 2011 or PIP.
- Implementing Authority: Special Purpose Vehicles.
- Sub-Schemes: ASDP (skill building), MKSP, DDU-GKY (youth training), SAKSHAM Centres.
