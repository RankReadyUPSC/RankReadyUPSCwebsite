## Salient Features

- Improves groundwater management in water-stressed areas of 7 states (Rajasthan, UP, Haryana, MP, Gujarat, Maharashtra, and Karnataka).
- 50% funded by the World Bank and the rest through budgetary support.
- Beneficiaries: Water-stressed regions and communities.
- Implementing Authority: Central Ground Water Board.
