## Salient Features

- Extended till 2026 to ensure India is ODF+ (toilets with water and hygiene) and ODF++ (toilets with sludge and septage management).
- Focuses on Water+ for untreated wastewater management.
- Beneficiaries: All statutory towns.
- Tools: Swachh Survekshan, Meri Life Mera Swachh Shahar Campaign, National Behaviour Change Communication Framework.
