## Salient Features

- Uses GIS (Geographical Information System) and remote sensing for horticultural crop planning.
- Focus crops: Mango, Banana, Citrus, Potato, Onion, Chilli, and Tomato (7 Crops) in 12 states.  Beneficiaries: Farmers in 12 states.
- Implementing Authority: ISRO, Mahalanobis National Crop Forecast Centre (MNCFC).
