## Salient Features

- Provides attractive scholarships for PhD programmes in science, engineering, or technology.  Targets students at IITs, IISERs, IISc Bangalore, central universities, and NITs.
- Beneficiaries: PhD students at premier institutions.
- Implementing Authority: National Coordinating Institute.
