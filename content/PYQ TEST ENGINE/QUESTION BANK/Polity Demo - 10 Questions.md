## Q001

What is the supreme law of India?

A. Parliament

B. Constitution

C. Supreme Court

D. President

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:**  
The Constitution of India is the supreme law of the country.

---

## Q002

Who is the constitutional head of the Union?

A. Prime Minister

B. Chief Justice of India

C. President

D. Home Minister

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:**  
The President is the constitutional head of the Union.

---

## Q003

Which Article deals with equality before law?

A. Article 14

B. Article 19

C. Article 21

D. Article 32

**Correct Answer:** A

**Explanation:**  
Article 14 guarantees equality before the law and equal protection of the laws.
