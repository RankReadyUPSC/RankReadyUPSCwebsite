## Salient Features

- SHCs are printed report card issued once in 3 years with crop-specific recommendations on 12 parameters for nutrients and fertilizers to improve productivity.
- Ensures judicious use of agricultural inputs.
- Beneficiaries: All farmers.
- Implementing Authority: Ministry of Agriculture.
