## Salient Features

- Promotes sustainable development in the fisheries sector through upgradation and creation of fisheries infrastructure.
- Focuses on bio-toilets, e-trading/marketing, aquaparks, and national brood banks.  Beneficiaries: Fish farmers, vendors, fisheries development corporations, SHGs, JLGs, Fish FPOs, and private firms.
- Initiatives: Matsya Sampada Jagrukta Abhiyan, River Ranching Programme, National Surveillance Programme for Aquatic Animal Diseases.
