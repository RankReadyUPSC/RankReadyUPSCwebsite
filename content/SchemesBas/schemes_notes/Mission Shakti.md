## Salient Features

- Focuses on women safety, including toll-free helplines, Nari Adalats, and Sakhi Niwas (hostels).
- Includes Samarthya (empowerment) and Sambal (safety/security) schemes.
- Beneficiaries: All women, with a special focus on distressed, marginalized, and trafficked women.
- Implementing Authority: Committee under Mission Shakti.
