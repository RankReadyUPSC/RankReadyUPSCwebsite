## Salient Features

- Replaced MEIS (Merchandise Exports from India Scheme).
- Refunds embedded taxes like electricity duty, VAT on fuel, mandi tax, and municipal taxes.  Beneficiaries: All sectors, including labor-intensive industries, merchant exporters, and manufacturer exporters.
- Implementing Authority: Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs (CBIC).
- Platform: ICEGATE.
