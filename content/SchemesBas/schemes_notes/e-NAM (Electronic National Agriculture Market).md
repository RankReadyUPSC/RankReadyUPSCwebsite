## Salient Features

- Creates a unified national market for agricultural commodities by integrating APMC mandis through an electronic trading platform.
- Facilitates trade through e-NWR (Negotiable Warehouse Receipt)
- Funded by the Agri-Tech Infrastructure Fund.
- Beneficiaries: Farmers, traders, and buyers.
- Implementing Authority: Small Farmers Agribusiness Consortium (SFAC).
