## Salient Features

- Promotes decentralized, farmer-friendly extension systems.
- Encourages women's participation with 30% reservation for beneficiaries.
- Beneficiaries: Farmers, Women Farmers, SHGs.
- Implementing Authority: Agriculture Technology Management Agency (ATMA).
