## Salient Features

- Aims for early detection of diseases, deficiencies, and developmental delays in children aged 0-6 years and students up to 18 years in government schools.
- Targets adolescent health and well-being.
- Beneficiaries: Children and adolescents aged 10-19 years.
- Implementing Authority: NHSRC, NIHFW.  Key Initiatives: Saathiya App, National Adolescent Health Strategy.
