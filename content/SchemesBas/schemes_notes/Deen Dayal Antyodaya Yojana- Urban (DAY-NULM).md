## Salient Features

- Empowers urban poor households by linking them to SHGs.
- Provides interest subsidies for SHG-bank linkages (additional 3% for women SHGs repaying on time).
- Supports skilling, micro-enterprise development, and credit for street vendors.
- Beneficiaries: Urban poor, SHGs, and street vendors.
- Implementing Authority: NSDC, Respective Ministries.
- Tools: UNDP-DAY-NULM, NIPUN, PaiSA Portal.
