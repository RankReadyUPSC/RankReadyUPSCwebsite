## Salient Features

- Promotes an Intellectual Property Rights (IPR) culture in the Indian defence manufacturing ecosystem.
- Beneficiaries: Defence PSUs (DPSUs), ordnance factories, armed forces, MSMEs, and startups.  Implementing Authority: Directorate General of Quality Assurance.
