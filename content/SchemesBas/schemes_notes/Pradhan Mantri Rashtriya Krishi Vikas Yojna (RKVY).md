## Salient Features

- Incentivizes states to increase public investment in agriculture.
- Offers grants of ₹5 lakh at the idea stage and ₹25 lakh at the seed stage.
- Beneficiaries: States increasing agricultural expenditure.
- Implementing Authority: State Sanctioning Committees.
