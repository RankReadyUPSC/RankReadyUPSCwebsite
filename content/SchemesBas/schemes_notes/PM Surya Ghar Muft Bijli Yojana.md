## Salient Features

- Supports installation of grid-connected rooftop solar (RTS) systems for residential sectors with central financial assistance.
- Develops one model village per district to promote rooftop solar systems in rural areas.  Beneficiaries: Indian citizens with rooftops suitable for solar panel installation, valid electricity connections, and no prior solar subsidy.
- Implementing Authority: National Programme Implementation Agency.
