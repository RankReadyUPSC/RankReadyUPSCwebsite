## Salient Features

- Develops infrastructure and provides basic amenities in identified Minority Concentration Areas.
- Allocates 80% of resources to priority sectors and 33-40% for creating assets for women/girls.  Beneficiaries: Minority communities in all districts.
- Key Tools: PMJVK Bhuvan (geo-tagging assets).
