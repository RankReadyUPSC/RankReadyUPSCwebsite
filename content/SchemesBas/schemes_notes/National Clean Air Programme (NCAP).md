## Salient Features

- Aims to reduce PM10 levels by up to 40% or achieve national standards by 2025-26 (base year 2017).
- Covers 131 cities in 24 states.
- Beneficiaries: Identified cities with poor air quality.
- Implementing Authority: Central Pollution Control Board.
- Tools: AIQ Management Plan, PRANA Portal, Indoor Air Pollution Monitoring & Management.
