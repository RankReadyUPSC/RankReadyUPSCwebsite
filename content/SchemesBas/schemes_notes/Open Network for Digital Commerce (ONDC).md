## Salient Features

- Democratizes digital commerce by shifting from platform-centric to open network models.
- Ensures interoperability using open-source protocols.  Beneficiaries: MSMEs, consumers.  Implementing Authority: Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT).
