## Salient Features

- Aims to rejuvenate the Ganga basin with components like Nirmal Ganga, Aviral Ganga, and Arth Ganga.
- Funded by the World Bank under a five-year loan.
- Beneficiaries: States along the Ganga basin.
- Implementing Authority: National Mission for Clean Ganga.
- Sub-Schemes: Ganga Gram Yojana, Clean Ganga Fund, PRAYAG Platform.
