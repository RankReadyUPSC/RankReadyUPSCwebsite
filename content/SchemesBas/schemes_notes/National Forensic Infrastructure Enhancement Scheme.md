## Salient Features

- The objective of the scheme is to meet the country's heightened demand for Forensic Science Laboratories (FSL).
- Includes establishment of campuses of the National Forensic Science Universities, Central Forensic Lab and Enhancement of existing infrastructure of Delhi Campus of the NFSU
