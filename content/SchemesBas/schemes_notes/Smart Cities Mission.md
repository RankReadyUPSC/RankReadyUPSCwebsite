## Salient Features

- Promotes core infrastructure, clean environment, and integrated Command and Control Centres (ICCCs) for services like traffic management and health.
- Encourages PPP models.
- Beneficiaries: Cities selected based on equitable criteria of urban population and statutory towns.
- Implementing Authority: Special Purpose Vehicles.
