## Salient Features

- Caps airfare at ₹2,500 for 500-600 km flights.
- Supports airlines through Viability Gap Funding (VGF).
- Focuses on regional connectivity in Tier-II/III cities and rural areas.
- Beneficiaries: Common citizens in underserved regions.
- Implementing Authority: Airports Authority of India.
- Sub-Schemes: Lifeline UDAN, Krishi UDAN, International Air Connectivity Scheme.
