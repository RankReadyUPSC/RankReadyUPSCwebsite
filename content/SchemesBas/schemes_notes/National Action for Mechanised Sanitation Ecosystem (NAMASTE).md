## Salient Features

- Promotes mechanized cleaning of sewers and septic tanks.
- Provides rehabilitation for manual scavengers and hazardous cleaning workers.
- Geo-tags sewers and septic tanks and develops a data management system.
- Beneficiaries: Urban sanitation workers and para-statal bodies.
- Implementing Authority: National Safai Karamchari Finance and Development Corporation (NSKFDC).
