## Salient Features

- Provides free immunization against 12 diseases, including Diphtheria, Tetanus, Polio, Measles, and Hepatitis B.
- Targets Rotavirus diarrhoea, Pneumococcal Pneumonia, and Japanese Encephalitis in specific regions.
- Beneficiaries: Indian citizens.
- Implementing Authority: Immunization Division of MoHFW.
- Key Tools: U-WIN Portal, eVIN.
