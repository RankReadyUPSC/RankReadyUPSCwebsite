## Salient Features

- Nutrition support for children, pregnant/lactating women, and adolescent girls in aspirational districts and North-East regions.
- Focuses on strengthening Anganwadi centers (Saksham Anganwadis), diet diversity, and mandatory millet supply.
- Beneficiaries: Children (6 months-6 years), pregnant/lactating women, and adolescent girls.  Implementing Authority: District Magistrates.
