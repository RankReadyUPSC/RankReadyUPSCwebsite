## Salient Features

- A non-lapsable reserve fund to finance health schemes like Ayushman Bharat, National Health Mission, and disaster preparedness.
- Funded through Health and Education Cess (4% on income tax).
- Beneficiaries: Schemes like AB-PMJAY and PMSSY.
