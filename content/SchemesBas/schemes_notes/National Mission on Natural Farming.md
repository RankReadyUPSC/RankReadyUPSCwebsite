## Salient Features

- Provides financial assistance of ₹15,000/ha/year for three years for the creation of on-farm input production infrastructure.
- Promotes cluster-based natural farming practices and aims to set up 15,000 Bio-input Resource Centres (BRCs).
- Beneficiaries: Farmers, FPOs, SHGs.
- Implementing Authority: National Steering Committee, chaired by the Agriculture Minister.
