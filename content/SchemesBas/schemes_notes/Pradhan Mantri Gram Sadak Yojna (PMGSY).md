## Salient Features

- Provides all-weather road connectivity with necessary culverts and drainage structures to unconnected habitations in rural areas.
- Includes RCPLWEA (for Left Wing Extremism-affected areas).
- Beneficiaries: Rural habitations.
- Tools: eMARG (GIS-based governance solution).
