## Salient Features

- World’s largest health insurance scheme covering ₹5 lakh per family annually for secondary and tertiary care hospitalization.
- Funded under the National Health Mission.
- Beneficiaries: SECC households, ASHA workers, senior citizens, and families under Rashtriya Swasthya Bima Yojana.
- Implementing Authority: National Health Agency.
