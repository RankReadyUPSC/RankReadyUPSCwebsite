## Salient Features

- Provides ₹1 lakh crore in loans with 3% interest subvention.
- Credit guarantee for loans up to ₹2 crores to promote agricultural infrastructure.  Beneficiaries: Farmers, FPOs, PACs, SHGs- Projects identified for providing supply chain infrastructure for cluster of crops, Projects promoted by Centre/State/Local Governments, Organic Inputs/Bio stimulant production units and for Infrastructure for smart and precision agriculture.
- Implementing Authority: NABARD.
