## Salient Features

- The scheme aims to foster economic growth and industrialization in the North Eastern Region (NER) of India.
- Capital Interest and Investment Incentive is provided for Plant machinery and construction for both Greenfield and Brownfield units.
- Manufacturing and Services linked Incentive is applicable to new units
