## Salient Features

- Designed to offer Blockchain-as-a Service. It is part of the broader National Blockchain Framework (NBF) provided under the National Strategy on Blockchain.
- Other Initiatives include-
- •NBFLite- Is a Blockchain sandbox platform, developed especially for startups/academia for rapid prototyping of applications, carrying out research and capacity building.
- • Praamaanik:  A solution that harnesses Blockchain technology to verify mobile app origins and is powered by the NBF.
- • National Blockchain Portal: It is developed on the theme based on Content Management System to manage the contents related to the NBF initiative.
