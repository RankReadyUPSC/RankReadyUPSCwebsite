## Salient Features

- A G2C (Government-to-Citizen) portal for agricultural marketing information.
- Includes initiatives like the AGMARKNET portal, strengthening of grading facilities, and marketing infrastructure.
- Beneficiaries: Farmers, FPOs (Farmer Producer Organizations), Cooperatives.
- Implementing Authority: Directorate of Marketing and Inspection, NIC, SFAC.
