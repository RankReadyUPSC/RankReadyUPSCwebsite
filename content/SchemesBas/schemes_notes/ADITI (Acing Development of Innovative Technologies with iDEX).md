## Salient Features

- Develops around 30 deep-tech critical and strategic technologies.
- Provides grant-in-aid up to 50% of the product development budget, capped at ₹25 crore per winner.
- Partnered with iDEX-incubators registered in India.
- Beneficiaries: Startups, MSMEs, and Indian companies.
- Implementing Authority: Defence Innovation Organization.
