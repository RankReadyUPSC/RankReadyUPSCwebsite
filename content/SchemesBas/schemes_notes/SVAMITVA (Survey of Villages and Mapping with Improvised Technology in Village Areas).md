## Salient Features

- Creates accurate land records for rural planning and property dispute reduction.
- Uses drones for large-scale mapping and establishes Continuous Operating Reference Stations (CORS) for geo-referencing.
- Beneficiaries: All rural villages.  Implementing Authority: NIC, Survey of India.
- Tools: DigiLocker, Gram Manchitra, SVAMITVA Dashboard.
