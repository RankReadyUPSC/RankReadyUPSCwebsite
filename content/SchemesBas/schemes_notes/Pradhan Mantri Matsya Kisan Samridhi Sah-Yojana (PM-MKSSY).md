## Salient Features

- Total outlay of ₹6,000 crore.
- Funding: 50% from public finance (World Bank and French Development Agency) and 50% from the private sector.
- Promotes adoption of aquaculture insurance.
- Beneficiaries: Fishers, aquaculture farmers, fish vendors, SHGs, FPOs, and startups.  Implementing Authority: Department of Fisheries.
- Sub-schemes: National Fisheries Digital Platform.
